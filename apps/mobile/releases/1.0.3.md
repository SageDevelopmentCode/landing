# Sage Field — Version 1.0.3

**Release Date:** May 2026

---

## What's New

### Tuition & Billing Cards
The Tuition & Billing screen has been rebuilt to give you a proper overview of each child's payment status. Instead of a simple list of admin requests, you'll now see a card for each program your child is enrolled in — Summer Program Tuition, Extended Learning, Friday Enrichment Day, and School Year Tuition — each showing what's been paid so far.

### Switch Between Children on the Billing Screen
If you have more than one enrolled child, a tab row at the top of the billing section lets you switch between them and see each child's individual payment status.

### Pay Tuition Directly in the App
You can now complete tuition payments without leaving the app. Tap any billing card — Summer Program, Extended Learning, Friday Enrichment Day, or Homeschool Drop-In — select your weeks or sessions, then choose your payment method and complete checkout natively via Stripe. Credit/debit card and ACH bank transfer are both supported. An optional processing fee toggle lets you cover the Stripe fee if you prefer.

### Teacher Profile — Cleaner Program Labels
In a teacher's student list, program tabs now display in a more readable format — "☀️ Summer 2026" and "🎒 School Year 26-27" — and the Summer tab is selected by default when the profile loads.

### Tuition & Billing on the Home Screen
Your program cards now appear directly on the Home screen — Summer Tuition, Extended Learning, Friday Enrichment Day, and Homeschool Drop-In — each showing your current payment status at a glance. Tap any card or the "View all" link in the section header to jump straight to the full Tuition & Billing screen.

### Tap to Configure Your Programs
Billing cards for Summer Program, Extended Learning (Aftercare), and Friday Enrichment Day now open a detail sheet directly in the app when tapped. You can review your program info without leaving the billing screen.

### Homeschool Drop-In Day Selection
The Homeschool billing card now opens a day-selection sheet where you can pick which days (Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu — up to 3 per week) you want for each week of the program. Weeks are displayed as actual date ranges (e.g., May 26–28, Jun 2–5) so it's easy to see exactly what you're choosing.

### Welcome Tour
The first time a parent opens the Home screen, a 5-slide onboarding tour appears automatically — covering the School Calendar, messaging teachers, Tuition & Billing, and a "You're All Set" closing slide. Parents can swipe between slides, use the arrow buttons, or skip entirely. Once dismissed it never appears again.

### Get Started Checklist
A checklist icon in the top-right of the Home screen tracks your onboarding progress. Tap it to open a sheet with 8 setup tasks — adding your child's photo, meeting teachers, sending a first message, setting up tuition, and more — each with a direct link to that feature. A progress bar shows how many tasks remain; the badge disappears once everything is complete.

### Pay for Siblings in One Transaction
If you have more than one enrolled child with unpaid Summer weeks, a banner in the checkout flow lets you add siblings to the same payment. A single Stripe transaction covers all selected children, with the receipt broken down by child. Only one processing fee is charged for the combined amount.

### Payment Breakdown Before You Pay
The payment confirmation step now shows a full order summary before you confirm — each child's name, program, weeks or months selected, and their individual amount. Switching between Credit/Debit Card and ACH Bank Transfer updates the estimated processing fee in real time. The Pay button requires you to acknowledge the fee via a checkbox before it activates.

### Help on Tuition & Billing
A help icon (?) in the top-right corner of the Tuition & Billing screen opens an FAQ and support page directly in the app.

### Community Channels on Home Screen
Your joined channels now appear directly on the Home screen. Tap any channel to jump straight into it without going through the Messages tab first.

### Community Channels
The Messages screen now has a Channels tab alongside your direct conversations. Browse available group channels, see member counts, and join the ones you want — unread badges keep you up to date at a glance.

### Rich Channel Messaging
Inside a channel you can send text, images, and file attachments. Consecutive messages from the same sender are grouped together, and date separators keep long conversations easy to navigate.

### Emoji Reactions
Tap any message to react. Six quick-picks (❤️ 🎉 🙌 👀 💡 ✅) are one tap away, or open the full emoji keyboard to find exactly what you're looking for. Reaction counts update in real time and show who reacted.

### Markdown in Feed Posts
Post detail views now render formatted markdown — bold, italics, headers, and lists display as intended rather than as raw symbols.

### View Channel Members
Tap the member count in a channel header to see everyone in that channel — their name, role (Parent or Teacher), and, for each parent, the children associated with their account. Members without a profile photo are shown with an initial avatar.

### Notification Bell
A bell icon in the top-right corner of the Home screen shows a red badge with your total unread count across all conversations. Tap it to open a notification sheet listing your most recent unread direct messages and channel messages — each with a preview, unread count, and timestamp. Tap any item to jump straight to that conversation, or tap "View all messages" at the bottom to open the full Messages screen.

## Staff Updates

- **Exclude Students from Attendance** — Students flagged with a "Don't Include" admin tag are now automatically hidden from the attendance roster, keeping the list clean without manual filtering.
- **Attendance — Week Plan View** — A new Week Plan tab shows a week-by-week headcount grid for the program. Each day displays stacked student avatars (up to 3, with a +N overflow) grouped by program (Summer, Aftercare, Field Fun Fridays). Left/right arrows step through weeks; tapping the week label opens a picker to jump directly to any of the 12 program weeks.
- **Attendance — Student Profile Sheet** — Tapping any student in the attendance list opens a detailed bottom sheet with three tabs: **Info** (legal name, grade, DOB, learning profile, regulation strategies, health flags), **Contacts** (Guardian 1 & 2 with phone and email, in-state and out-of-state emergency contacts), and **Notes** (staff notes by category — general, behavioral, academic, social, health — each with a color-coded badge).
- **Attendance — Improved Marking** — Attendance toggles now include haptic feedback. The system records who marked a student and when, and respects enrollment/payment status for Summer day eligibility.
- **Attendance — Pickup Tracking** — Staff can mark a student as Picked Up and record who collected them — name and relationship — along with the exact pickup time. Pickup details can be edited after the fact if corrections are needed.
- **Create & Manage Channels** — Teachers can create new channels (with an optional description) directly in the app and join or leave any channel from within the channel view. Real-time subscriptions keep the channel list and unread counts current.
- **Removed: Clock In / Clock Out** — The Clock In and Clock Out action buttons have been removed from the staff interface.

## Internal Notes

- Stock card images compressed from up to 32 MB down to ~100 KB each using WebP — originals deleted from the repo.
- Transaction queries now correctly scoped to the signed-in parent's `parent_id`, fixing a data isolation bug in the previous build.
- Replaced the admin-initiated `pending_payment_requests` card UI with the full application-based billing card system; pending requests are still accessible on the web.
- Production build channel configured in `eas.json`.
- Fixed a duplicate week-counting bug where a parent with both a full-plan and individual weekly payment transactions would see "15 of 12 weeks paid" on the Summer Tuition card. Week numbers are now deduplicated via Set in both `home.tsx` and `tuition.tsx`.
- New `src/lib/channel-actions.ts` module centralizes all channel messaging operations: send, edit, delete, react, media upload (Supabase `message-images` / `message-files` buckets), and real-time subscriptions. Added `getChannelMembers()` and `getChannelMemberCount()` to power the member list sheet; each member record includes their profile and associated children.
- Community channels backed by Supabase tables: `messaging.channels`, `messaging.channel_members`, `messaging.channel_messages`, `messaging.message_reactions`.
- Unread counts tracked per-member via `last_read_at`; new users are auto-enrolled in default channels on first load.
- Onboarding checklist progress stored in the parent's `onboarding_checklist` record; the welcome tour is gated on the `intro_seen` flag so it fires exactly once per account.
- Sibling payments group all selected students under a single Stripe PaymentIntent; the receipt includes an itemized breakdown by child with individual amounts.
- Attendance records now store `marked_by` (staff user ID) and `marked_at` timestamp alongside the student and program identifiers.
- Summer attendance records extended with `pickup_time`, `picked_up_by_name`, and `picked_up_by_relationship` fields to support the new pickup tracking feature.
- Added a markdown rendering dependency; feed post detail views now use a `MarkdownBody` component instead of plain text.
