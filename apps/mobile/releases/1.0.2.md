# Sage Field — Version 1.0.2

**Release Date:** May 2026

---

## What's New

### Welcome Slideshow

When you first open the app, you'll now see a friendly welcome slideshow that introduces key features and helps you get started quickly.

### Floating Navigation Bar

The bottom navigation bar has been redesigned to float above the screen content, giving the app a cleaner and more modern feel.

### More Room When Filling Out Forms

The navigation bar now automatically hides while you're filling out any form, giving you more screen space to focus on what you're entering.

### Events on the Home Screen

Upcoming events are now displayed right on your home screen so you always know what's coming up.

### Comments Now Visible in the Feed

Comments on posts in the feed are now shown properly — you can read and follow conversations without any issues.

### Better Handling for Non-Enrolled Users

If your account isn't linked to an active enrollment yet, you'll now see a clear, helpful screen explaining next steps instead of a blank page.

## Staff Updates

- **Summer Attendance** — Staff can now track attendance for summer programs directly in the app.
- **Student Ordering & Calendar** — The staff attendance view includes improved student ordering and a more reliable calendar.
