# Sage Field — Version 1.0.5

**Release Date:** May 2026

---

## For Parents

### Share Your Feedback

A new "Share Your Feedback" card appears at the bottom of the Home screen, below the Refer a Family section. Tapping "Share Feedback" opens a dedicated Feedback screen where parents can:

- Rate their overall experience (1–5 stars)
- Select affected areas from 12 category pills (multi-select): Home & Dashboard, My Children, Tuition & Billing, Messages, Calendar, News Feed, Enrollment, Forms & Documents, Volunteer, Emergency Contacts, Navigation & Layout, Other
- Write an optional message (up to 2,000 characters)
- Opt in to follow-up contact

After submitting, a confirmation screen appears with a "Done" button that returns to Home.

---

### Editable Student Profiles

Parents can now edit their child's profile directly in the app.

**Learning tab** — Tapping "Edit" on the Learning Overview card opens a sheet with fields for Learning Style, Special Interests, Strengths & Interests, and Current Challenges.

**Profile tab** — All four sections now have an "Edit" button:

- **Medical Info** — Yes/No toggles for Has Allergies, Has Medical Conditions, and Has Emergency Medications, with a conditional description field when "Yes" is selected
- **Learning Profile** — Learning Style, Special Interests, and Strengths & Interests
- **Regulation** — Regulation Strategies and Dysregulation Response
- **Support** — Current Challenges

Each section saves independently. The Medical Info card also now displays Emergency Medications (previously missing from the UI).

---

### Activity Preferences

A new Preferences screen is accessible from the More menu. Parents can set per-child participation preferences for each upcoming published activity so teachers know how each student should be involved.

Avatar tabs at the top let parents switch between enrolled children. For each activity with food, parents choose one of three levels:

- **Watch only** — child observes but does not participate
- **Cook, no eat** — child interacts with ingredients but does not eat
- **Full** — child cooks and eats

An optional notes field is available per activity. Before saving, parents must check an allergen acknowledgment checkbox: "I have reviewed the ingredients and allergens listed above. I understand and acknowledge that Sage Field is not responsible for any allergic reactions, dietary sensitivities, or adverse responses related to food items consumed during activities." The checkbox resets when switching to a different child. A single "Save Preferences" button (disabled until acknowledged) commits all changes for the selected child.

The screen is accessible from the More menu under **Activities** (previously labeled "Preferences").

Only published, public activities are shown. During the summer program (May 26 – Aug 14, 2026), an activity only appears if the selected child has a completed payment covering that activity's date.

**Reminder card on Home** — An amber card now appears on the parent Home screen when there is a published activity on a paid day for any child that has no preference set yet. Tapping "Set Preferences" navigates directly to the Preferences screen. The card disappears once all applicable activities have preferences set.

---

### Summer Schedule Date Correction

All 12 summer week date ranges in the enrollment selection UI have been updated to reflect the correct 2026 calendar. Summer now runs May 26 – Aug 14, 2026 (previously showed incorrect dates from a prior year's template).

## For Staff

### Care Log

New Care Log feature accessible from the More menu (medkit icon). Teachers can record sunscreen and bug spray applications for enrolled students on a per-day basis. The list is scoped to weekdays only (Mon–Fri).

**Date navigation** — Prev/next arrows skip weekends automatically. Tapping the date label opens a calendar sheet (weekends grayed and non-tappable). A "Jump to Today" link appears when not on today's date.

**Search** — Full-width search bar filters the student list in real time.

**Batch mode** — Select Sunscreen and/or Bug Spray pills, then tap student cards to select them. "Select all" / "Clear" links appear. A floating submit button shows the count and logs one entry per student per activity in a single batch insert.

**Individual mode** — Each student card expands to show existing entries for the day (icon + label + timestamp). Two quick-log buttons, **+ Sunscreen** and **+ Bug Spray**, insert immediately with optimistic UI and a spinner while saving.

---

### Activities

New Activities feature accessible from the More menu (ribbon icon). Teachers can create, edit, publish, and manage learning activities — including food and ingredient details — for the summer program.

**Activity list** — Filter pills for All, Published, and Drafts (each with a count). Cards show thumbnail, title, excerpt, and status/visibility badges. FAB to create; pull-to-refresh; long-press or swipe to delete with confirmation.

**Activity detail** — Full-width image gallery, status and visibility badges, expandable food/ingredient sections, Edit FAB, and a Changelog button (clock icon) for edit history.

**Activity form** — Title, description, up to 10 photos (compressed before upload), Draft/Published toggle, Private/Public toggle, and an optional food editor with multiple foods per activity (name, allergen notes, image gallery, and per-ingredient entries). Navigating away with unsaved changes prompts a discard confirmation.

**Changelog** — Timeline of every edit with editor avatar (deterministic color or initials fallback), relative timestamp, and a bullet-point summary of what changed.

---

### Newsletters

New Newsletters feature accessible from the More menu (newspaper icon). Teachers can draft, edit, and publish weekly newsletters for parents.

**Newsletter list** — Filter pills for Drafts and Published (each with a count). Cards show title, week range, status chip, and relative timestamp. FAB to create; tap the ellipsis button to open or delete (with confirmation).

**Creating a newsletter** — A bottom sheet prompts for a title and a week range selected from a scrollable list of the next 16 Mon–Fri ranges. New newsletters are initialized with five default sections: Welcome Message, Class Updates, Upcoming Events, Parent Reminders, and Photo Gallery.

**Newsletter editor** — Edit the title, week range, and view mode (Traditional or Slideshow). Toggle each section visible or hidden with a switch. Tap any section row to open the full-screen section editor. A clock button opens the Changelog.

**Section editor** — Full-screen body text area with an image strip for uploading and deleting photos (compressed before upload). The Class Updates section is special: each teacher writes their own personal update, displayed alongside other teachers' entries for parents.

**Preview** — Shows the newsletter exactly as parents will see it in Traditional (scrolling section cards) or Slideshow layout.

**Publish** — Tapping "Publish" triggers a confirmation prompt, then marks the newsletter published and visible to all parents.

**Changelog** — Timeline of every edit: editor avatar (or deterministic color / initials fallback), relative timestamp, and a bullet-point summary of what changed.

---

### Staff Home — Today's Students & Attendance

The staff Home screen now shows a live list of enrolled students for the selected date and lets teachers log attendance, care, and pickups without leaving the screen.

**Today's Students list** — Shows all students with a paid enrollment (summer, aftercare, or Field Friday) for the selected date. Students tagged "Don't Include" are excluded. An allergy badge appears for students with allergies. A search bar filters the list in real time. Skeleton placeholders are shown while data loads.

**Date navigation** — Prev/next arrows shift the day. Weekends display a rest message instead of a student list.

**Per-student action panel** — Tapping a student card opens a bottom sheet with:

- **Mark Present** — toggles a row in `attendance.summer_records`; tap again to remove
- **Log Aftercare** — toggles a row in `attendance.aftercare_records`
- **Log Field Fri** — toggles a row in `attendance.field_friday_records`
- **Log Sunscreen / Log Bug Spray** — quick-log care entries with optimistic UI

**Pickup recording** — Once a student is marked present, a pickup section appears. Tapping it opens a two-step sheet: first select an authorized pickup person from the student's emergency contacts, then pick a pickup time (5-minute slots, 12 pm – 8 pm, defaults to the nearest 5 minutes). Saves the person and time on the attendance record.
